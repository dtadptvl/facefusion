3. MODEL METADATA CATALOG & LICENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application utilizes open-weights deep learning models for face processing.
In accordance with zero-bundle security and memory constraints, no model binaries
are bundled within the application binary. Models are dynamically cached upon
first use from authorized mirrors, verified against cryptographic checksums.

Below is the transparent catalog of supported models, their authors, licenses,
and official source repositories:

A. HyperSwap 1a 256 (hyperswap_1a_256)
   - Processor: Face Swapper
   - Vendor: FaceFusion
   - Year: 2025
   - Input: 256x256 RGB
   - License: ResearchRAIL (OpenRAIL for Research)
   - CRC32: 79e50d4b
   - Repository: https://github.com/facefusion/facefusion-assets

B. InSwapper 128 (inswapper_128)
   - Processor: Face Swapper
   - Vendor: InsightFace (DeepInsight)
   - Year: 2023
   - Input: 128x128 RGB
   - License: Non-Commercial (Strictly for academic & non-commercial evaluation)
   - CRC32: 7057c6ea
   - Repository: https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface

C. GFPGAN 1.4 (gfpgan_1.4)
   - Processor: Face Enhancer
   - Vendor: TencentARC (Tencent Applied Research Center)
   - Year: 2022
   - Input: 512x512 RGB
   - License: Apache-2.0
   - CRC32: 5a6c6364
   - Repository: https://github.com/TencentARC/GFPGAN

D. CodeFormer (codeformer)
   - Processor: Face Enhancer
   - Vendor: Shangchen Zhou (sczhou / S-Lab, Nanyang Technological University)
   - Year: 2022
